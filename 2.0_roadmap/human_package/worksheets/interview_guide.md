# Worksheet — Interview Guide (Step 08)

8–10 semi-structured interviews, 30–45 min each. Record (with consent) and
transcribe. Aim for 25–40 respondents across roles.

## Warm-up
1. What's your role, and what does a typical week look like?
2. What tools are in your memory/agent stack today?

## Core (when do they reach for mem20)
3. Tell me about the last time you needed an agent to "remember" something. What happened?
4. Where did it fail or surprise you?
5. How do you decide whether to *trust* a recalled memory?

## Grounded vs simulated (mem20's core idea)
6. If the system told you "this is a simulated/hypothetical memory, not a real one,"
   how would that change your behavior? Would you want to see that label?
7. Have you ever been burned by an AI confidently stating something untrue as fact?

## Integration & ops
8. How would mem20 fit your existing pipeline (stdio MCP? a proxy?)?
9. What would make you *stop* using it?

## Closing
10. If you had a magic wand, what's the one thing mem20 must nail for 2.0?
