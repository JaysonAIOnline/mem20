# RPG Recipe: Battle Axe / War Axe

**Prefix:** `AXE_`  

## Base Modeling
1. **Head** – Carefully modeled from a refined plane or curve profiles. Use Boolean for the eye (haft hole). Strong bevels on the cutting edge and back spike/beard.
2. **Haft / Handle** – Curve with slight taper + Curve to Mesh. Or subdivided cylinder reshaped.
3. **Langets / Metal Straps** – Thin strips running down the haft (Curve or Boolean).

## Geometry Nodes
- Rivets on the head and langets
- Leather wrap near the grip
- Optional engraved runes on the blade

## Materials
- Darkened steel or pattern-welded look for the head
- Wood haft
- Leather binding
