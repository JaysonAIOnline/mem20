# RPG / Fantasy Item Recipes (Expanded)

All items follow the **same advanced pipeline** principles.

## Weapons & Armor
- Longsword (`sword/`)
- Battle Axe (`axe/`)
- Longbow (`bow/`)
- Heater Shield (`shield/`)
- Knight Helmet (`helmet/`)
- Plate Armor Pieces (`armor/`)

## Magic & Containers
- Potion Bottle (`potion/`)
- Treasure Chest (`chest/`)
- Mage Staff (`staff/`)

## Environment
- Furniture – Table/Chair (`furniture/`)
- Common Props – Barrel, Crate, Banner, Torch (`props/`)

Use consistent prefixes (`SWD_`, `AXE_`, `BOW_`, `ARM_`, `PRP_`, etc.) and the standard collection hierarchy.
