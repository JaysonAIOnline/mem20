# World Bible - The Unreliable Prophecy
## Vertical Slice Edition (Quietvale + Bureaucracy Hills)

---

## 1. Setting Summary

A fantasy world overlaid with a living celestial bureaucracy. Ordinary villages exist alongside filing-cabinet landscapes, passive-aggressive forests, administrative hub cities, and archives of previous failed Chosen Ones. Destiny is documented, revised, and frequently misfiled.

The core irony: ancient evils require permits.

**Core Mechanic:** Paperwork as gameplay

- **Destiny Affairs** - "Resolved With Minimum Effort"

## 5. Tone Guide

**Keywords:** dry, institutional, bureaucratic, understated, reluctant, warm-under-the-absurdity

### Dialogue Style:
- Bureaucrat: "tired, precise, passive-aggressive"
- Guidebook: "flat, superior, institutional memory"
- Player: "reluctant, increasingly exasperated"
- Old Wizard: "cynical, tired, ancient mentor"

### Visual Pillars:
- Silhouettes: filing cabinets, stamps, binders, wrinkled robes
- Color: Warm Quietvale morning light vs institutional office-drone
- Materials: parchment, ink, wood, stone, metal stamps

### Audio Tone:
- Sparse, almost none in Quietvale
- Low office-drone in Hills
- Stamps as combat sound design
