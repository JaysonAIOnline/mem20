    Straight answers

    What actually happens when I pay?

    Stripe confirms the payment and sends a signed webhook. Provisioning runs from that webhook, not from your browser — so you get your service even if you close the tab. While provisioning is in dry-run mode, nothing is created, and the banner above will say so.

    Why are some products unpriced?

    A price is not invented to fill a gap. An unpriced product has no Stripe Price behind it, so the server refuses to sell it. You will see the exact reason on its card.

    Why do the prices and status change while I am looking?

    Because they are live. This page re-reads the server on a timer and pauses when the tab is hidden, so what you see is current rather than cached from whenever the page happened to load.
